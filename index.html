Services

What I take off and price

Commercial roofing

Membrane systems, insulation, tapers, edge metal, penetrations and details measured from the roof plans.

Asphalt

Shingle roofing and paving quantities, with waste, starter, ridge and underlayment broken out clearly.

Siding

Wall area net of openings, corners, trim, soffit and fascia, organized by elevation.

ACT ceilings

Acoustical tile and grid counts room by room from reflected ceiling plans, including perimeter and hangers.

How I work

From plans to proposal in four steps

01

Review the plans

I check drawings, specs and addenda, then flag gaps or conflicts before pricing starts.

02

Measure in Bluebeam

Markups are color-coded by system so every area and length can be audited later.

03

Build the estimate

Quantities turn into material lists, supplier-ready, with waste and accessories included.

04

Write the proposal

A clear scope, inclusions, exclusions and alternates your client can read in minutes.
